### Interview with S.W.

Interview: S.W.
Date Conducted: 9-6-2026

## The Workaround:

**Q1: How do you currently document your pokemon card collection?**

- "I create collections which are basically lists of my cards in an online app on my phone."

**Q2: How long does the process usually take you? (The Cost)**

- "Like an hour. It takes awhile because I have to search each card by name individually and a long list appears."

**Q3: What is the most annoying part of the process?**

- "Whenever I search a card by name, I find so many with the same name, it takes awhile to find my specific card."

**Q4: How do you currently fix the problem?**

- "I have to try different search parameters like changing the name or searching with the unique set number, but the set number on that app doesn't always work."

**Q5: What is the single most important thing that could make your overall experience better?**

- "It should be better designed to take in the set number by default."

## Other applicable quotes:

- "Other apps dont have every single card that I search for in any list."

- "TCG Player does not have good tools for collection formatting, I cannot permanently put my favorite cards at the top of the list."

**When asked: Does TCG Player (Primary Competition) work well in all environments?**

- "Yes, but it takes internet connection and does not work well on my computer, it does not even show my collections that I made on my phone."

- "I wish it was like a social media platform that you could put your collections as viewable to other people and format them like binders to look cool."
